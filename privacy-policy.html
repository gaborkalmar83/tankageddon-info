TANKAGEDDON

Privacy policy. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Tankageddon is a mobile tank game made by Kalmarium Services B.V., the Netherlands ("we").

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, scrap, upgrades, achievements, daily streak), a saved game in progress and your settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-up, and none of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any personalised ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy choices. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The one-time Premium purchase removes all ads, and with them all ad-related data collection in the game.

Purchases

Tankageddon Premium is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid, so the game can remove ads and add the upgrade points. We never see your payment details.

AI-assisted content

The game's art and audio were created with the help of AI tools. The game itself does not send anything you do to an AI service and contains no AI chat or AI-generated content created while you play.

Children

Tankageddon is not directed at children under 13. It contains cartoon combat. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Website visits

This website counts page views with a cookie-free script that stores nothing on your device and sends only the page address, the referring website and the country (derived from your network address, which is not kept). It is switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy and use the Privacy choices option in the game's settings.
