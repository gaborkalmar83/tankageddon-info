Questions

Can I play Tankageddon offline?

Yes. The whole campaign plays without internet. Ads and purchases need a connection, the game does not.

Is Tankageddon free?

Yes. It is free to play with a small banner ad. You get 3 lives per map, and a short ad can give you 3 more. Tankageddon Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads for good, gives 99 lives per map, 10 upgrade points and 10 extra sound packs. There are no loot boxes.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones, foldables and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer. The game plays in landscape.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved on your device.

Does Tankageddon support gamepads?

Yes. Since version 1.3.6 you can play with a Bluetooth or USB gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo and more). The left stick drives, the right stick aims and fires, and the Settings Controller section lets you set dead zones, aim response, invert, swap sticks and remap every button.

How many levels and bosses are there?

99 levels in 10 worlds, and every level ends with a different alien boss, 99 in total.

Is it a top-down tank game?

Yes. You drive with one thumb and aim and fire with the other, from a top-down view, with three control layouts you can move and resize.

Was AI used to make the game?

Art and audio were created with the help of AI tools. Design, code and balancing are done by the developer.
