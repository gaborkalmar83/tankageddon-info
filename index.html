The last tank on Earth

In 2091 the aliens came. In 40 days every army on Earth fell. One tank survived. Drive it with one thumb, aim and fire with the other, and fight your way from a fallen city to the heart of the alien Hive. Tankageddon is a top-down tank game with real weight to it, a full campaign and a different boss at the end of every map.

99 boss fightsDrone motherships, pod launchers, laser striders, rail-cannon crabs, burrowing worms, shield fortresses and the Hive Overmind.
A real campaignStorm alien bases, clear infested zones, escort supply trucks and free captured humans before the boss shows up.
Roll over themAlien soldiers have big red eyes, green blood and a healthy fear of your treads.
Upgrade your tankCannon, armour, shield, engine, missiles and scope. Grow from one barrel to a quad-barrel battery.
Steal alien techDisintegrator, railgun, missile swarm, golden shield and five kinds of special shells.
Day and night10 worlds from Fallen City to The Hive, in morning light, midday, evening or pitch-black night.

Questions

Can I play Tankageddon offline?

Yes. The whole campaign plays without internet. Ads and purchases need a connection, the game does not.

Is Tankageddon free?

Yes. It is free to play with a small banner ad. Tankageddon Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads for good.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones, foldables and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved on your device.
