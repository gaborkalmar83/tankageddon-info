TANKAGEDDON

Top-down tank shooter: controls, bosses and upgrades

In 2091 the aliens came, and in 40 days every army on Earth fell. One tank survived. This page covers how Tankageddon plays: the controls, the missions, the bosses and the upgrades.

Twin-stick controls

You drive the tank with one thumb and aim and fire with the other, from a top-down view. There are 3 control layouts, the sticks can be moved and resized, and you can set how visible they are: always, on touch or never. The game plays in landscape on phones, foldables and tablets. Other options include 4 difficulty levels from relaxed to brutal, 5 zoom levels, screen shake and minimap settings.

Missions before every boss

A level is more than a boss arena. Before the boss shows up, you complete missions, such as:

  • Destroy the alien bases
  • Clear the infested zone
  • Secure the crashed chopper
  • Escort the convoy
  • Destroy the comm spires
  • Hold the position
  • Hunt the elite squads
  • Free the captured humans

Alien soldiers have big red eyes and green blood, and they have a healthy fear of your treads. You can run them down with the tank, flatten their cars and smash through barricades.

99 levels, 99 boss fights

Every level ends with a boss, and the 99 bosses have a name each: drone motherships, pod launchers that rain soldiers on you, laser striders, rail-cannon crabs, burrowing worms, shield fortresses and the Hive Overmind itself. Learn their tells, break their weak points and survive their rage.

Ten worlds, day and night

The campaign crosses 10 worlds: Fallen City, Burning Fields, Red Canyon, Iron Harbour, Frozen Outpost, Green Hell, Refinery, Black Marsh, Ash Lands and The Hive. Each can be played in morning light, blazing midday, burning evening or pitch-black night, lit only by your headlights and muzzle flashes.

Upgrade your tank

Collect scrap and upgrade the cannon, armour, shield, engine, missiles and scope. You grow from one barrel to a quad-barrel battery. You can also load special shells: armour-piercing, high explosive, incendiary, cluster and canister. Power-ups show what your tank can become, from an alien disintegrator that melts enemies into green ash to overdrive armour, a railgun, a missile swarm and a golden shield.

Tankageddon is free to play with a small banner ad and works offline. If you prefer a controller to the touch sticks, read about gamepad support.

Questions

How do the controls work?

You drive with one thumb and aim and fire with the other, from a top-down view, with three control layouts you can move and resize.

What can I upgrade?

You upgrade the cannon, armour, shield, engine, missiles and scope with scrap, and you can load special shells such as armour-piercing, high explosive, incendiary, cluster and canister.

How many worlds and bosses are there?

99 levels in 10 worlds, and every level ends with a different alien boss, 99 in total.
